XionChat is a sovereign messaging app that works without central servers.

Your identity is your cryptographic key (DID). Nobody can take it from you.

FEATURES:
- Noise IK end-to-end encryption
- UDP hole punching — works behind CGNAT
- No phone number or email account required
- Encrypted P2P groups
- Federated beacons for redundancy
- 100% open source

More info: https://iap2p.uk/

"Sovereignty is not asked for. It is compiled."
